## The courts and the registry
The High Court sits in Kampala, and the Uganda Registration Services Bureau keeps the trademark register there.

### Questions
How long does a trademark registration last in Uganda?
Seven years from the filing date. It can then be renewed for ten years at a time.
Who can file a trademark?
Any person or company. The Bureau accepts filings made through a registered trademark agent.
Can a business outside Uganda register a trademark here?
Yes. A foreign applicant files through an agent registered in Uganda.
Which courts do advocates appear in?
Magistrates' courts, the High Court, the Court of Appeal and the Supreme Court.
